What warranty do your tents have?
All TrailLite tents carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects in poles, zips and seams. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, fire or normal wear of the waterproof coating. The Summit Pro 4 expedition tent has an extended 5-year warranty.

How waterproof is the TrailLite 2?
The TrailLite 2 flysheet has a hydrostatic head rating of 3,000 mm and the groundsheet is rated at 5,000 mm. It weighs 1.9 kg packed.

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping within the continental United States takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $75. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $19. We currently ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom.

Do you offer repairs?
Yes. Our Denver workshop repairs tents, backpacks and sleeping bags from any brand. A standard zip replacement costs $35 and takes about 10 business days.

What fuel does the EmberCamp stove use?
The EmberCamp stove uses standard screw-thread isobutane-propane canisters. It boils 1 litre of water in about 4 minutes at sea level.

How do I contact customer support?
Customer support is available by chat on our website from 8:00 to 20:00 Mountain Time, Monday to Saturday.
