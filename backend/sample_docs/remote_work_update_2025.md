# Remote Work Policy Update — 2025

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT — Lumenfield Outdoor Supply Co. is an invented company. Memo dated 15 February 2025 from the People Team.

## What is changing

Starting 1 April 2025, employees in office-eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week, an increase from the previous allowance. Teams must still keep at least one shared "anchor day" in the office each week, chosen by the team lead.

## Home office stipend

Each office-eligible employee may claim a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk, chair or monitor. Claims require a receipt and must be submitted through the expenses system before 31 December 2025.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.
