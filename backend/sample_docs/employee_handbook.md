# Lumenfield Outdoor Supply Co. — Employee Handbook

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT — Lumenfield Outdoor Supply Co. is an invented company used to demonstrate KnowledgeBridge AI. Edition: January 2024.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, plus the company's 9 public holidays. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on contracted hours. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

PTO requests of more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your line manager.

## Remote work

Employees in office-eligible roles may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Remote days must be agreed with your team lead and recorded in the shared team calendar. Store and warehouse roles are not eligible for remote work.

## Expenses

Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. The daily meal allowance during business travel is $55. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in standard cities and $260 per night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London). Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts.

## Onboarding

New hires complete a 10-day onboarding programme. Week one covers product training on our tent, backpack and stove ranges; week two is spent shadowing a colleague in your own department. Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy for their first 90 days.

## Equipment discount

Employees receive a 35% discount on all Lumenfield products, limited to $2,000 of purchases per calendar year.
